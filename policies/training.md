# Required Training

## Compliance training
All new hires must complete security awareness, data privacy and code of conduct training within their first 30 days. Each course takes about 45 minutes in the learning system.

## Role training
Your manager assigns role-specific training during your first week. Progress is tracked in the learning system.

## Onboarding buddy
Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from their team for the first 90 days. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and helps you meet people.

## 30-60-90 day check-ins
Managers hold check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days to review goals, training progress and any questions.
