# Benefits Enrollment

## Enrollment deadline
New hires must enroll in benefits within 30 days of their start date. If you miss the 30-day window, you can only enroll during the annual open enrollment period or after a qualifying life event.

## Coverage start date
Medical, dental and vision coverage starts on the first day of the month after your start date. If you start on the first day of a month, coverage starts that day.

## Medical plans
Example Co. offers two medical plans: a PPO plan and a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) with a health savings account (HSA). The company contributes 500 dollars per year to the HSA for employees enrolled in the HDHP.

## Dental and vision
Dental and vision plans are optional and can be added for yourself, your spouse or partner, and dependents.

## Retirement plan
Employees can join the 401(k) plan from their first paycheck. Example Co. matches 100 percent of the first 4 percent of salary you contribute. The match vests immediately.

## Qualifying life events
Marriage, birth or adoption of a child, and loss of other coverage are qualifying life events. You have 30 days after the event to change your benefit elections.
