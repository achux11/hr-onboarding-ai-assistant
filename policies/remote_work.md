# Hybrid and Remote Work

## Hybrid schedule
Most roles are hybrid. Employees work in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday and may work remotely on Monday and Friday.

## First two weeks
New hires work in the office every day for their first two weeks to meet their team and complete onboarding sessions.

## Fully remote roles
Fully remote work is allowed only for roles posted as remote. Changing to fully remote requires approval from your manager and HR.

## Home office stipend
Hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 300 dollars, claimed through the expense system within 60 days of the start date.

## Working from another country
Working from outside your home country, even for a short period, requires HR approval in advance because of tax and legal rules.
