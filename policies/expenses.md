# Travel and Expenses

## Submitting expenses
Submit expenses in the expense system within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt for any expense over 25 dollars.

## Approval and reimbursement
Your manager approves expenses, and approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll run.

## Meals during travel
When traveling for work, meals are covered up to 75 dollars per day.

## Booking travel
Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.
