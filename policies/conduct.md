# Code of Conduct and Workplace Concerns

## Respectful workplace
Example Co. expects every employee to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

## Reporting a concern
You can report a concern to your manager, to HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Reports are reviewed confidentially and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

## Workplace accommodations
Employees who need a workplace accommodation for a disability, pregnancy or religious practice should contact HR. HR handles each request individually and confidentially.

## Dress code
The dress code is business casual in the office. Teams that meet customers may have additional guidelines.
