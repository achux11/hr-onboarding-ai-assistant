# Payroll

## Pay schedule
Employees are paid every two weeks on Friday. If payday falls on a holiday, pay is issued on the previous business day.

## First paycheck
Your first paycheck arrives on the first regular payday after you complete one full pay period. Depending on your start date, this can be 2 to 4 weeks after you start.

## Direct deposit
Set up direct deposit in the HR system during your first week. Until direct deposit is active, a paper check is mailed to your home address.

## Tax forms
Complete your federal W-4 form in the HR system before your first payday. You can update your withholding at any time.

## Pay stubs
Pay stubs are available in the HR system on each payday and are kept there for 7 years.
