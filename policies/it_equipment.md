# IT Equipment and Accounts

## Laptop
Every new hire receives a company laptop on day one. IT ships laptops to remote hires 3 business days before their start date.

## Accounts and login
Your email, chat and HR system accounts are created before your first day. IT sends login instructions to your personal email the day before you start.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be set up on your first day before you can access company systems.

## Lost or stolen equipment
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT help desk within 24 hours so the device can be locked remotely.

## Software requests
Request additional software through the IT service portal. Most requests are approved within 2 business days.
