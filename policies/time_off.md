# Time Off Policy

## Paid time off accrual
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, earned at 1.25 days per month. New hires start accruing PTO on their first day of work. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Using PTO in the first 90 days
New hires may use up to 3 days of accrued PTO during their first 90 days. Requests longer than 3 days in the first 90 days need manager approval.

## Requesting time off
Submit PTO requests in the HR system at least 2 weeks in advance for absences of 3 or more days. Your manager approves or declines the request within 3 business days.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over to the next calendar year. Days above 5 expire on December 31.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 6 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick days can be used from the first day of work and do not require advance notice, but you should tell your manager as early as possible.

## Company holidays
Example Co. observes 10 paid company holidays each year. The holiday calendar is published in the HR portal every December.
