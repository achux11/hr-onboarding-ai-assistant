# Scope Items

The onboarding process broken into discrete scope items, each with a clear trigger, owner and output.

| ID | Scope item | Trigger | Owner | Key steps | Output | Assistant support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OB-01 | Pre-boarding | Offer accepted | HR + IT | Create accounts, ship laptop to remote hires, send login email | Accounts ready before day one | Checklist dates |
| OB-02 | Day One Setup | Start date | IT + Manager | Set up MFA, issue laptop, assign onboarding buddy | New hire can log in and has a contact | Answers IT and buddy questions |
| OB-03 | Payroll Setup | Week one | Employee + Payroll | Direct deposit, W-4 | Paid correctly on first payday | Answers pay questions with citations |
| OB-04 | Benefits Enrollment | Start date, 30-day window | Employee + Benefits | Choose medical plan, add dependents, 401(k) | Enrolled before deadline | Reminds of 30-day deadline |
| OB-05 | Learning & Compliance | Week one | Employee + Manager | Compliance courses, role training, 30/60/90 check-ins | Training done within 30 days | Checklist dates and training answers |
| OB-06 | Workplace Setup | Week one | Employee + Manager | Hybrid schedule, home office stipend | Set up to work in office and at home | Stipend and schedule answers |
| OB-07 | Employee Questions & Case Handoff | Any time | HR | Answer routine questions, route sensitive ones | Fast answers and safe handoffs | Guardrails and escalation |

## Out of scope

Compensation changes, visa and immigration questions, discipline and separation, and legal questions. The assistant routes these to HR (OB-07) and never answers them itself.
